This course transforms abstract concepts into solid technical knowledge, explaining step by step the path from the silicon atom and electric current to the graphical interface, global networks, and artificial intelligence.

## **Module 1: Hardware \- Building "Thinking" from Matter**

### **Session 1: The Processor (CPU) \- Architecture and Execution (7 Chapters)**

* **Chapter 1:** The Transistor \- The Switch That Changed the World.  
* **Chapter 2:** Von Neumann Architecture \- The foundation of any modern computer.  
* **Chapter 3:** Internal Units: ALU (Arithmetic Unit) and CU (Control Unit).  
* **Chapter 4:** Registers \- Ultra-fast memory next to the "heart".  
* **Chapter 5:** The Instruction Cycle (Fetch-Decode-Execute) \- In detail.  
* **Chapter 6:** Frequency, Cores and Threads.  
* **Chapter 7:** Manufacturing process (Nanometers) and Moore's Law.

### **Session 2: Memory Hierarchy and Buses (6 Chapters)**

* **Chapter 1:** RAM \- From capacitors to bits (DRAM vs. SRAM).  
* **Chapter 2:** Cache Memory (L1, L2, L3) \- The bridge over the speed bottleneck.  
* **Chapter 3:** Permanent Storage: HDD vs. SSD (SATA, NVMe, NAND Flash).  
* **Chapter 4:** Virtual Memory and Paging \- How to expand RAM to disk.  
* **Chapter 5:** ROM and BIOS/UEFI \- The first breath of life of the machine.  
* **Chapter 6:** Motherboard and Chipset \- Data highways (Buses, PCIe, USB).

## **Module 2: Digital Logic and Basic Software**

### **Session 3: The Binary Universe and Logic Gates (6 Chapters)**

* **Chapter 1:** Binary and Hexadecimal System \- How machines count.  
* **Chapter 2:** Text Representation: ASCII, UTF-8 and Unicode.  
* **Chapter 3:** Representation of Images and Sound (Pixels, Colors, Sampling).  
* **Chapter 4:** Basic Logic Gates (AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR, XOR).  
* **Chapter 5:** Adders and Logic Circuits \- How to physically add 1 \+ 1\.  
* **Chapter 6:** Boolean Algebra \- The mathematics behind digital decisions.

### **Session 4: The Operating System \- Chaos Management (6 Chapters)**

* **Chapter 1:** What is an Operating System? The role of mediator between humans and hardware.  
* **Chapter 2:** The Kernel \- The Invisible Heart (User Space vs. Kernel Space).  
* **Chapter 3:** Process Management \- How the OS divides CPU time (Scheduling & Context Switching).  
* **Chapter 4:** Memory Management \- Allocation, protection and Garbage Collection.  
* **Chapter 5:** File System (NTFS, ext4, APFS) \- How we organize bits on the disk.  
* **Chapter 6:** Drivers and Interrupts \- How the OS communicates with peripherals.

## **Module 3: From Code to Image (Software & Graphics)**

### **Session 5: Programming Languages, Compilation and Execution (6 Chapters)**

* **Chapter 1:** Levels of Abstraction \- From Assembly to High-Level Languages.  
* **Chapter 2:** Compiler vs. Interpreter \- Translation into machine code.  
* **Chapter 3:** Virtual Machines and Hybrid Languages (JVM, CLR, JIT Compilation).  
* **Chapter 4:** Anatomy of a Running Program: Stack and Heap.  
* **Chapter 5:** Classic Runtime Errors: Stack Overflow, Memory Leaks and Null Pointers.  
* **Chapter 6:** Software Life Cycle (From source code to distribution).

### **Session 6: Graphics, Peripherals and Interface (5 Chapters)**

* **Chapter 1:** GPU vs. CPU \- Why do we need massively parallel computing?  
* **Chapter 2:** Anatomy of a Screen Image: Pixels, Framebuffer and Refresh Rate.  
* **Chapter 3:** 3D Rendering, Shaders and Ray Tracing \- How we build virtual worlds.  
* **Chapter 4:** Input Peripherals \- Convert physical actions into digital signals.  
* **Chapter 5:** Digital Audio \- From sound wave to analog signal and PCM.

## **Module 4: The Interconnected World and Modern Technologies**

### **Session 7: Networks, Internet and Protocols (7 Chapters)**

* **Chapter 1:** What is a Network? The OSI Model and the TCP/IP Stack explained simply.  
* **Chapter 2:** IP Addresses (IPv4 vs. IPv6) and MAC Address \- Identity on the Network.  
* **Chapter 3:** DNS (Domain Name System) \- The phone book of the Internet.  
* **Chapter 4:** The Data Road: Packets, Routers, Switches and Submarine Cables.  
* **Chapter 5:** Web protocols: HTTP/HTTPS and TCP vs. UDP.  
* **Chapter 6:** Network Security: Firewall, VPN and DDoS Attacks.  
* **Chapter 7:** Wireless Transmission: Wi-Fi, 5G and Fiber Optics.

### **Session 8: Cloud, Cryptography and Artificial Intelligence (6 Chapters)**

* **Chapter 1:** What is the Cloud? (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS and Virtualization/Containers).  
* **Chapter 2:** Cryptography \- How we protect data (Symmetric vs. Asymmetric Encryption / RSA).  
* **Chapter 3:** SSL/TLS Certificates and Digital Signature.  
* **Chapter 4:** Databases (SQL vs. NoSQL) \- Where the world stores big data.  
* **Chapter 5:** Fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence \- Neural Networks and Data Modeling.  
* **Chapter 6:** The Big Picture \- What happens in the millisecond you press Enter in a browser.
