## **Topics Covered**

* **Focus:** The Interconnected World and Modern Technologies
* **Chapter 1: What is a Network?:** The OSI Model and the TCP/IP Stack explained simply.
* **Chapter 2: IP Addresses and MAC Address:** IPv4 vs. IPv6 and MAC Address – Identity on the Network.
* **Chapter 3: DNS (Domain Name System):** The phone book of the Internet.
* **Chapter 4: The Data Road:** Packets, Routers, Switches and Submarine Cables.
* **Chapter 5: Web Protocols:** HTTP/HTTPS and TCP vs. UDP.
* **Chapter 6: Network Security:** Firewall, VPN and DDoS Attacks.
* **Chapter 7: Wireless Transmission:** Wi-Fi, 5G and Fiber Optics.
